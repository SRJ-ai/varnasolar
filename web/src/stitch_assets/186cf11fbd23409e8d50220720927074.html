Architectural Precision Meets Megawatt Scale.

The Warangal facility represents a paradigm shift in industrial utility-scale solar deployment. Engineered to maximize yield in a challenging, high-irradiance environment, this 12MW installation demonstrates VARNASOLAR's commitment to turnkey EPC delivery without compromising on structural integrity.

Every component, from the deep-piled foundational arrays to the automated single-axis trackers, was meticulously modeled to ensure seamless grid synchronization and optimized energy yield over a projected 30-year lifecycle.
